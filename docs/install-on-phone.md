# Install JIEN on your phone

## iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the production JIEN URL in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Keep the name **JIEN**, tap **Add**, then open JIEN from the new home-screen icon.
4. Open JIEN once while online so the offline app shell and your local records are ready.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the production JIEN URL in Chrome and sign in.
2. Open the Chrome menu, then tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm **Install**, then open JIEN from the new home-screen icon.
4. Open JIEN once while online so the offline app shell and your local records are ready.

When an update banner appears, tap **Refresh** to load the new build. Locally stored workouts and food remain on the device.
